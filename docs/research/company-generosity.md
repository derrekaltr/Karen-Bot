## Top findings

1. **Regulatory fixed-cash claims are the biggest, most certain payouts** on
   qualifying routes: Canada APPR $400/$700/$1,000; EU261 €250/€400/€600;
   US involuntary denied boarding up to $1,075/$2,150.
2. **American Airlines** has the best-documented goodwill for non-elites
   (~5k–15k miles; 12.5k–20k for Executive Platinum). Best channel:
   customer-relations email form.
3. **Delta** has sent proactive 10,000 SkyMiles to non-elites after
   cancellations; live chat beats phone for small items.
4. **United** is status-tiered; May 2026 example: $175 travel certificate or
   8,750 miles for a transatlantic turnback.
5. **Southwest and JetBlue publish formulaic amounts**: Southwest minimum $75
   voucher on request (controllable 3h+ delay, through April 2027, never
   automatic); JetBlue flat 5,000 TrueBlue points (automatic, account must be
   attached pre-disruption).

Tactics: (1) claim formal entitlements first, then a short specific written
goodwill request citing documented costs; (2) escalate customer relations →
executive email → DOT complaint (forces a written reply in 60 days);
(3) telecom/banks: retention queue / "just ask" (reported fee-waiver success
~90%, self-reported data).

Corrections to web lore: JetBlue's old $50–$250 ladder is retired (flat
5,000 points since early 2026). DOT withdrew the proposed $200–$300 delay-
compensation rule in 2025 — US airlines owe NO cash for delays, only
refunds/rebooking.

## 2. Channel effectiveness (cross-industry)

National Customer Rage Study (CCMC/ASU), 2025 wave (n=1,000): 77% had a
problem; channel mix 45% email/chat/social vs 33% phone; 43% of public
social complaints get NO response; 40% satisfied with resolution; 2020 wave:
>50% of complainants get NOTHING. 2015: monetary remedy alone 37% satisfied
vs monetary + apology/explanation 73% — ALWAYS pair the ask with dignity.

Which? (UK 2021, n=5,053): live chat/face-to-face rated most effective
(67%), phone/email 60%, chatbot ~50%, social <50%, letter 40%.

U of Toronto (4M airline tweets): airlines respond more when the tweet
mentions loyalty-program membership — Karen should cite status where real.

Executive escalation (elliott.org): verified contacts, NO published success
rate ("not a promise of a reply"); etiquette matters — escalate only after
lower levels fail (mass-blasting burns the channel; abuse risk for us).

## 3. Airlines

### Formal entitlements (PG — encodable)
- US denied boarding (14 CFR 250, eff. 2025-01-22): 200% of one-way fare
  capped $1,075 (1–2h late), 400% capped $2,150 (2h+); no comp if rebooked
  within 1h. Mishandled-baggage liability floor $4,700 domestic.
- DOT automatic refund rule (eff. 2024-10-28): automatic cash refund for
  cancellation/significant change (>3h dom, >6h intl, airport change, added
  connections, downgrade); bag fee refund after 12h; 7 business days (card).
- EU261: €250/€400/€600 by distance, 3h+ arrival delay (June 2026 reform
  deal keeps amounts; not yet law).
- Canada APPR: large carriers $400 (3–6h) / $700 (6–9h) / $1,000 (9h+);
  small $125/$250/$500; within-control, written claim within 1 year;
  vouchers only with consent and must exceed cash value.
- DOT complaint timelines: acknowledge 30 days, substantive reply 60 days
  (verify 14 CFR 259.7).

### Carrier-published commitments (PG)
- JetBlue: 5,000 TrueBlue points for controllable delay >3h or cancellation
  <4h notice; automatic within 24h; TrueBlue account required pre-
  disruption; $12 meal voucher; Canada/Europe excluded.
- Southwest (DOT settlement, through April 2027): transferable voucher ≥$75
  for 3h+ controllable arrival delay, ON REQUEST ONLY.
- Alaska: ≥2,000 miles or ≥$50 code for 3h+ interruption (secondary source;
  VERIFY at alaskaair.com).
- Delta: no published delay entitlement; eCredits last 5 years; Bags-on-Time
  2,500-mile reward removed 2026-03-30 (single source, verify).

### Goodwill norms (JR/FC — targeting only)
- American: 5k–15k miles non-elite; 12.5k–20k Exec Plat; customer-relations
  email form; miles post on approval; DOT complaint produced 10k in one case.
- Delta: 2.5k–10k miles; proactive 10k after cancellation (non-elite, 2023);
  live chat > phone (Thrifty Traveler); feedback form for claims 7+ days old.
- United: In-the-Moment Care app (must ask); proactive only for 6h+ delays;
  2026: $175 ETC or 8,750 miles; 2023 meltdown 30k by application.
- Southwest: 25k points in Dec 2022 meltdown; otherwise the $75 settlement
  voucher path.
- No internal comp matrices surfaced. (2017 Delta memo — agents to $2,000,
  supervisors to $9,950 — is VOLUNTARY BUMP authority, not complaint
  goodwill; do not use.)

## 4. Hotels
- Marriott Ultimate Reservation Guarantee (PG, marriott.com): walked guests —
  luxury $200 + 90k points (all elites); select $100 (+90k for Plat+);
  economy $50 (+45k Plat+); Ritz/St. Regis $200 + 140k (Titanium/Ambassador);
  room-type guarantee $25–$100; lounge unavailable 750 pts/night.
- Hilton: Diamond $200 cash if 48h-guaranteed paid reservation not honored
  (2015-era source — VERIFY current terms).
- Hyatt: no fixed payouts; goodwill 10k–30k points reported (FC).
- Typical complaint goodwill ~10k–15k points (FC); corporate loyalty care
  beats the property when the property stonewalls.

## 5. Everyday consumer
- Amazon: returnless-refund eligibility algorithmic/unpublished (reportedly
  <$75 items); $309.5M returns class settlement (Jan 2026); refund-abuse
  enforcement active.
- Uber: in-app within 30 days, "sole discretion" (PG); Lyft: credits over
  refunds (JR, low).
- DoorDash: report within 24h; credits usual; refund-abuse flags/deactivation.
- Instacart: $60M FTC settlement (Dec 2025) over credits-instead-of-refunds;
  report within 4 days; "unusual refund activity" disqualification.
- Returns generosity (CR 2024): most lenient Nordstrom, Costco (no time
  limit); strict: Best Buy (15% restocking + $45 phones), GameStop, L.L.Bean
  fees. No numeric index exists.

## 6. Banks & telecom
- LendingTree "just ask" (self-reported, directional): annual-fee waiver
  success 90–95%; late fee ~89–92%; APR cut 76–83%; limit increase ~84%.
  30% of non-askers didn't know they could ask.
- Retention offers (Aug 2026): Amex Platinum 50k points no-spend; Gold 20k;
  CSR $250; Amex most generous, Chase rare; Amex retention via chat works.
- Telecom: ~2/3 negotiate, 76% of those succeed (CR, dated); retention queue
  via cancellation menu; Spectrum anecdote $68→$45/mo x12; AT&T Guarantee
  (Jan 2025, PG): automatic full-day credit for fiber 20min+/wireless 60min+
  outages; Verizon $20 app credit after Jan 2026 outage.
